# SEO content checklist

SEO helps people find the restaurant through search. Most of the work is already handled by the website. The restaurant team only needs to keep the information clear, accurate, and complete.

## Fill this in once

Open **Sanity → Site Settings** and complete:

- Address
- Opening times in French and English
- Opening hours for search engines
- Telephone number
- Price range, such as `€` or `€€`
- Instagram address
- Contact form recipient

Opening hours for search engines use short day names and a 24-hour clock:

```text
Mo-Fr 11:30-21:30
Sa-Su 12:00-22:00
```

Use more than one line when different days have different hours.

## Fill this in on every page

Open **Pages**, select a page, then open **SEO and social sharing** near the bottom.

### Search title

This is the clickable title people may see on Google.

- Write a different title for each page.
- Say clearly what the page contains.
- Mention Paris where it reads naturally.
- Keep it short—about one brief sentence.
- Fill in both French and English.

Examples:

```text
French: Bánh mì vietnamien à Paris
English: Vietnamese bánh mì in Paris
```

If this is empty, the normal page title is used.

### Search description

This is the short text Google may show below the title.

- Explain what the visitor will find.
- Use natural language rather than a list of repeated search words.
- Keep it to one or two short sentences.
- Fill in both French and English.

If this is empty, the normal page subtitle is used.

### Social sharing image

This image may appear when the page is shared in messages or on social media.

- Use a clear landscape image.
- The best size is `1200 × 630` pixels.
- Avoid important details close to the edges.
- Use a different image only when it helps explain that page.

If this is empty, the page's main image is used.

## Images throughout the website

When adding an image, fill in the French and English description.

Describe what is visible and useful. Do not begin with “image of” or “photo of.”

Good:

```text
Bánh mì au porc grillé, carottes marinées et coriandre
Grilled pork bánh mì with pickled carrots and coriander
```

Avoid file names, repeated search words, or descriptions that do not match the picture.

## Before publishing

- Read the French and English text.
- Check that names, address, opening times, and prices are current.
- Check every button opens the correct place.
- Check every image has a useful French and English description.
- Publish the page.
- Wait a few minutes for the website to update. Ask the site administrator for help if the published change still does not appear.

## Keep Google consistent

The address, telephone number, opening times, website address, and menu should match in:

- Sanity
- the live website
- Google Business Profile
- Instagram and delivery services

Conflicting information can confuse visitors and search services.
